Predictive Scoring Methodology

Acadivance calculates the student success score deterministically:

S = 0.35A + 0.20T + 0.15L + 0.20P + 0.10E
  • A (35%): Academic Performance (CGPA scaled 0–100, penalized by backlogs).
  • T (20%): Classroom Attendance percentage.
  • L (15%): LMS course completion & quiz performance.
  • P (20%): Placement readiness, coding tests, and aptitude assessments.
  • E (10%): Campus engagement, clubs, and mentor check-ins.

Missing components are re-weighted fairly rather than assigned a zero penalty.
